# Module 1

## Overview

This module introduced systems analysis and design and the Systems Development Life Cycle (SDLC). I explored the role of the systems analyst and how different phases of the SDLC contribute to developing information systems that meet business and user needs.

## Key Concepts

- Systems analysis and design
- Systems Development Life Cycle (SDLC)
- Role of the systems analyst
- Business and user needs
- System development methodologies
